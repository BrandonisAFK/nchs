# Reposition New Covenant Home Services

## Goal
Turn the site into a professional company overview led by direct home buying and cash offers, supported by repair and property maintenance services. Remove the Home Watch identity, appointment booking, exact pricing, and aggressive lead-capture language.

## Changes
- Rewrite the homepage headline and supporting copy around three clear areas: buying homes directly, property repairs, and general maintenance.
- Replace Home Watch and concierge-focused service cards with concise home acquisition, repair, painting, exterior care, and maintenance information.
- Remove the pricing section from the homepage and services page.
- Rewrite the “Why New Covenant” section so it supports both sellers and service customers without unsupported promises.
- Create a dedicated Contact page and make “Contact” the consistent primary action in the header, homepage, services page, and footer.
- Keep the simple contact form and direct phone/email options; remove the multi-step booking flow and booking form.
- Redirect the old `/book` address to `/contact` so old links do not break.
- Rewrite the services page as a shorter, two-column overview rather than a long Home Watch checklist.
- Update titles, descriptions, social tags, structured data, sitemap, redirects, and form definitions to match the new business focus.
- Preserve existing painting and property-maintenance location pages because those services are still offered, while removing conflicting Home Watch language sitewide.

## Technical details
- Use the existing navy, gold, logo, typography, and reusable page sections.
- Keep Netlify Forms for contact messages and remove the obsolete booking form definition.
- Keep useful local SEO pages indexed; remove `/book` from the sitemap and add `/contact`.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, links, form behavior, and the production build before publishing.
- Run the required security check, then request publication of the finished site.
